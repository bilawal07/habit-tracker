HotFire Habits — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Summary: HotFire Habits is a 100% offline-first daily habit tracker. No account, no login, no ads, and no personal data is sold or uploaded to any developer server.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

All habit titles, daily completion check-ins, streak history, earned badges, and app preferences (reminder mode, Night Owl Grace, theme) are stored locally on your device inside an on-device SQLite database and Android SharedPreferences.

2. Local Backups & Android Auto Backup

If you choose Export Backup (.json), you control where that file is saved or shared. Android Auto Backup may also back up app data to your personal Google Drive account according to your Android system backup settings.

3. Notifications & Permissions

Notification, exact alarm, boot-completed, and optional battery-optimization permissions are used solely to deliver your scheduled local habit reminders (6:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 8:00 PM, 11:00 PM) and keep them active after device restarts.

4. Anonymous App Analytics (Optional Release Telemetry)

Release builds may collect anonymous, aggregated usage and crash stability metrics (such as app opens and feature usage counts) without collecting your habit titles, personal identity, email, or account data.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete individual habits inside the app at any time or clear all data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android Settings.
